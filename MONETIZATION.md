# Monetization sequence

1. Publish useful, verifiable comparisons and make the source and disclosure policy visible.
2. Research each provider's currently open official affiliate program for the owner's actual region. Apply only after the owner reviews the agreement. We have not applied, accepted terms or invented commission rates.
3. After approval, put the real tracking destination in the I-Lang provider row. Test the destination and disclosure. Use recurring/revenue-share programs only when that specific program's written terms actually provide them; managed hosting is not automatically recurring commission.
4. Register Search Console and collect real impressions/clicks. Keep traffic provenance and provider payout statements. Do not manufacture activity or referrals.
5. Consider clearly labeled sponsorships later only if there is real audience demand. Keep evidence-based ordering independent of payments.
6. A future sale can include the owned domain, code, brand assets and documented financial history, subject to each service's transfer rules. No revenue or valuation is guaranteed; a free hosting subdomain itself is not owned domain property.

The initial site uses direct official links. No X/Facebook accounts or posts are created. If social publishing is added later, generate posts only from verified offers and label their eligibility and expiry; public posting requires the owner's account authorization and platform-compliant access.
